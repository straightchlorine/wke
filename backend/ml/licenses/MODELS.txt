The wke-ml image contains the weights of two models, downloaded from Hugging
Face at build time at the revisions pinned by MINILM_REV and DISTILBART_REV in
the Dockerfile:

- sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
  https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
- sshleifer/distilbart-cnn-6-6
  https://huggingface.co/sshleifer/distilbart-cnn-6-6

Both are licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0, included here as
Apache-2.0.txt. The build converts the distilbart weights from
pytorch_model.bin to model.safetensors; the weight values are not changed.
